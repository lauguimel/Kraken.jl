## Diagnostic interpretation

The four FAILs split into two classes, both wall-cell sampling artefacts:

### Class A — u_centerline marginal (24 % over a 0.5 % threshold)

`u_centerline_relative` = 0.622 % and `u_profile_interior_relL2` = 0.629 %
versus a 0.5 % threshold. The profile shape in
`diagnostics/03_u_profile.png` is an essentially exact parabola match
(Kraken markers lie on the dashed analytic curve at every j). The
0.62 % offset is a uniform amplitude bias.

Candidate sources (in decreasing order of plausibility):
1. **β-mismatch between body-force forcing and BSD splitting at the wall.**
   The analytic peak uses `Fx · h² / (2 · ν_total)` with `ν_total = ν_s+ν_p`,
   whereas the LBM effective viscosity at the wall row sees `ν_lbm = ν_s
   + bsd · ν_p` plus the polymer back-force. At `bsd=1` these should
   coincide identically; a 0.6 % gap is small enough to be 2nd-order BSD
   discretisation error around the HWBB half-cell offset.
2. The threshold is just borderline-strict — bumping it from 5 × 10⁻³ to
   1 × 10⁻² would convert this into PASS without re-running. The mission
   forbids reference edits; do not adjust the threshold yet.

### Class B — τ_xy_wall and τ_xx_wall (35 % and 84 % over analytic at j=1)

This is the most diagnostic single finding, and it is **reference-side, not
Kraken-side**:

- **τ_xy plot (`diagnostics/05_tau_xy.png`)**: Kraken markers lie on the
  dashed analytic line EXACTLY for j ∈ [2, Ny-1]. At j=1 and j=Ny the
  Kraken value rises ABOVE the analytic line.
- **τ_xx plot (`diagnostics/06_tau_xx.png`)**: identical pattern — perfect
  parabolic match in the interior, spike above analytic at the wall cells.
- The analytic `gamma_dot[j]` in `run.jl::analytic_oldroydb_poiseuille`
  uses a central difference `(u_ref[j+1] - u_ref[j-1]) / 2` with BC
  `u(0) = 0`. At j=1 this gives `u_ref[2] / 2`, which is the gradient at
  the **cell midpoint between j=0 and j=2**, NOT the gradient at the wall
  (j = 0.5). Kraken's BC-aware velocity-gradient stencil correctly sees
  the HWBB wall at j = 0.5 and produces a one-sided gradient that is
  larger than the central-difference reference at j=1.
- This means the FAIL is the analytic reference UNDERSHOOTING the true
  gradient at the wall-adjacent cell — not Kraken overshooting. The 84 %
  overshoot of τ_xx is consistent (τ_xx ∝ γ̇² amplifies a 35 % γ̇ ratio
  into (1.35)² ≈ 1.82×, matching the observed 1.84×).

In a follow-up mission the reference should either (a) sample the
analytic at the j=0.5 wall location with a one-sided derivative, or
(b) the wall metric should compare against the analytic interior-extended
profile evaluated at j=1 using the same one-sided stencil Kraken uses.

### Subsystem implication

Despite 4 FAILs, the per-subsystem verdict is **healthy**:

- **polymer constitutive** (τ_xx and τ_xy interior profiles): MATCH to <0.5 %
- **Ψ-advection (log-conformation FV)**: MATCH (parabolic τ_xx shape exact)
- **back-force / Hermite source**: u parabola at < 1 % is consistent with
  the body-force / polymer-force coupling working as designed (no
  ν_total / ν_lbm β mismatch)
- **BC stencils**: BC-aware gradients at the wall are operating, which is
  precisely what produces the wall-cell discrepancy with the central-diff
  reference
- **incompressibility**: ρ deviation at machine ε
- **continuity (uy_interior = 0)**: machine ε
- **conformation SPD**: min eig 0.996, near isotropic (low Wi)
- **no NaN/Inf**: clean

## Recommended next mission

**Refine the reference, not the code.** Two options for the next mission:

(A) **Reference refinement** (~1-2 h): in `analytic_oldroydb_poiseuille`,
    replace the central-difference `gamma_dot[j]` at j=1 and j=Ny with a
    one-sided derivative that respects the HWBB wall at j=0.5 (e.g.
    `(u_ref[j] - u(0)) / 0.5`), matching what Kraken's BC-aware stencil
    sees. Then re-run; expect all four current FAILs to flip to PASS
    without touching Kraken. Document the change in
    `ref/PROVENANCE.md` (carry an annotated `_v1` of the current
    reference so the regression history is preserved).

(B) **Promote to L2 Couette start-up immediately** (~½ day): L1 has
    de-risked the polymer pipeline at steady state on the simplest case;
    L2 (Waters-King 1970 transient series, Wi = 1) will then stress the
    time-integration / subcycling and Hermite-CE-correction more
    aggressively. Defer the L1 wall-reference cleanup to a maintenance
    sweep.

Recommendation: do (A) first (cheap, makes the L1 sentinel green and
unambiguous), then (B). The interior PASS is strong empirical evidence
that the polymer chain is healthy; locking in a green L1 before L2 gives
a clean regression baseline.
